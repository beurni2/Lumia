N1-CORE-CLEAN-AUDIT verdict = FIX_NEEDED, narrow scope.

Root cause:
`region=nigeria + languageStyle=clean` and internal `region=nigeria + languageStyle=null/undefined` can reach generic `core_native` high-energy-rant templates from `voiceClusters.ts`, including patterns like:

`i ${actionPast} the ${anchor} AGAIN. AGAIN!!!`

Confirmed surfaced sample:
- cohort: internal `ng_null`
- source: `core_native`
- hook: `i dodged the lockscreen AGAIN. AGAIN!!!`
- scanner hit: `exclamation_pile:!!!`

Audit also found a milder `ng_clean` sample from the same template family:
- `the inbox is dodging me. AGAIN.`

The defect exists because:
1. `coreCandidateGenerator.ts` does not gate high-energy-rant template selection by region/languageStyle.
2. Western generic-template demotion has regexes that would catch this family, but those demotions are gated away from `region=nigeria`.
3. Nigerian style penalty only applies to `pidgin` / `light_pidgin`, so `clean` and `null/undefined` are not protected.
4. `PIDGIN_MOCKING_PATTERNS` are QA-only today and not applied in this core_native runtime path.

Product distinction:
- `ng_pidgin`: Nigerian, funny, culturally grounded, natural pidgin allowed.
- `ng_light_pidgin`: Nigerian, funny, culturally grounded, light pidgin allowed.
- `ng_clean`: Nigerian, funny, culturally grounded, clean English / Nigerian English. No pidgin/mock-pidgin/shouty artifacts.
- `languageStyle=null/undefined`: internal safety fallback only. No pidgin/mock-pidgin/shouty artifacts or forced Nigerian decoration.
