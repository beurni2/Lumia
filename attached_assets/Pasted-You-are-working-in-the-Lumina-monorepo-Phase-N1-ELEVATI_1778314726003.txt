============================================================
QUALITY STANDARD
============================================================

Each Nigerian idea should be evaluated as a complete unit:

HOOK:
- must have a fast Nigerian comedic contradiction
- should sound natural, not translated
- should feel like something a Nigerian creator/viewer recognizes
- should not rely only on slang
- should not feel like a Western hook skeleton with Nigerian nouns pasted in
- should be catchy enough for a first 1–3 second video opening

WHAT TO SHOW / SCENARIO:
- must describe a full visible skit
- should have setup, action, turn, payoff
- should be easy to visualize in one read
- should be shootable in a room, office, kitchen, street-adjacent, car, shop, or everyday Nigerian setting
- should make the Nigerian reality visible through behavior, not explanation
- should not be abstract or purely emotional
- should not introduce a new premise unrelated to the hook

HOW TO FILM:
- must be specific to the skit
- should tell the creator what to keep visible
- should include the key action and ending/payoff
- should be 1–2 sentences
- should be shootable in 10–15 minutes
- must not reuse generic boilerplate
- must not say useless things like “Keep the care centered”
- must not overuse:
  - phone-level lock-off
  - desk-height lock-off
  - soft daylight, one take
  - Keep the X centered
- must not use banned phrases:
  - phone on a tripod
  - single static shot
  - single locked-off shot
  - locked-off on tripod
  - let the joke land
  - let the silence land
  - film yourself reacting
  - creator reacts
  - show the X on camera

CAPTION:
- should add a second comedic beat
- should not simply repeat the hook
- should sound like a Nigerian creator caption, but not forced
- should be short, punchy, and natural

NIGERIAN CULTURAL QUALITY:
The idea should understand Nigerian realities and humor, including areas like:
- WhatsApp/group chat behavior
- transport fare / danfo / ride decisions
- POS/bank app/salary anxiety
- data/recharge/card issues
- light/generator/freezer/fridge realities
- family/auntie/uncle pressure without lazy stereotypes
- office/intern/work stress
- church/event/social obligation energy
- snacks/food/budget contradictions
- “I said I’m coming” but the body/money/weather said otherwise
- exaggeration, teasing, avoidance, and stress-turned-comedy

Avoid lazy crutches:
- wahala
- abeg
- omo
- jollof
- NEPA
- village people
- my enemies
- generic Lagos traffic jokes

These can appear only if they are genuinely specific and funny, not as default punchlines.

============================================================
CLASSIFICATION SYSTEM
============================================================

For every approved Nigerian entry, assign one of these classes:

A. PRESERVE_STRONG
Entry is strong enough. Do not rewrite unless part of a future style-diversity pass.

B. HTF_ONLY
Hook, scenario, and caption are good, but `howToFilm` is boilerplate or too generic.

C. SCENARIO_PLUS_HTF
Hook is usable, but `whatToShow` is too thin/generic/abstract, and `howToFilm` also needs rewrite.

D. HOOK_PLUS_SCENARIO_PLUS_HTF
Hook is weak or generic, scenario does not save it, and filming guidance is boilerplate.

E. FULL_UNIT_REWRITE
Hook, scenario, filming guidance, and caption all need rewrite as one complete idea unit.

F. STEREOTYPE_RISK_REWRITE
Entry contains stereotype-risk or banned cultural crutch usage that should be rewritten or removed.

G. ABSTRACT_ANCHOR_FIX
Entry has an abstract anchor that creates broken filming guidance or weak scenario grounding.

H. PIDGIN_TIER_CANDIDATE
Entry could be converted or duplicated into a stronger `pidgin` tier candidate later, but do not rewrite now.

An entry may have multiple tags, but choose one primary class.

============================================================
WHAT TO DETECT
============================================================

Flag entries with:

1. Boilerplate `howToFilm`
Examples:
- `Phone-level lock-off, soft daylight, one take.`
- `Desk-height lock-off, soft daylight, one take.`
- `Counter-level lock-off, daylight, one take.`
- `Keep the {anchor} centered.`
- any repeated filming line that could fit dozens of unrelated ideas

2. Broken abstract-anchor filming
Examples:
- `Keep the care centered.`
- `Keep the peace centered.`
- `Keep the energy centered.`
- `Keep the vibe centered.`
- `Keep the lie centered.`
- `Keep the damage centered.`

3. Weak hooks
Examples of patterns to flag:
- generic anthropomorphic object line with no cultural specificity
- Western internet phrase with Nigerian noun pasted in
- too long / awkward rhythm
- too plain to stop scroll
- too abstract to film
- too much “X don expose Y” if repeated without a fresh twist
- obvious first-draft phrasing

4. Weak scenarios / whatToShow
Flag if:
- no visible payoff
- no turn
- action is too abstract
- reads like a premise summary, not a skit
- Nigerian reality is named but not dramatized
- could fit any country after swapping one noun

5. Weak captions
Flag if:
- repeats hook
- generic
- not a second beat
- too Western
- too forced-pidgin
- too explanatory

6. Stereotype/cultural-risk entries
Flag:
- NEPA as lazy punchline
- jollof as lazy punchline
- village people
- my enemies
- auntie/uncle if used as a flat stereotype
- any mock-pidgin or tourist-coded framing

7. Pidgin/light-pidgin mismatch
Flag if:
- `light_pidgin` is too heavy
- `pidgin` is too light or functionally nonexistent
- wording feels cartoonish or unnatural
- grammar sounds imitated
