TASK 1 — Audit E-1: W2-N repeated howToFilm prefix

Audit:
artifacts/api-server/src/lib/westernHookPackBatchNext2.ts

Find every W2-N entry whose howToFilm starts with:
“Phone on a tripod, one locked-off shot.”

Report:
- total affected entries
- sample 20 affected entries
- whether the rest of the howToFilm is specific enough
- whether only the prefix is weak
- whether any full howToFilm lines are low-quality
- whether the issue appears in W2-L or W2-T too
- whether this affects first impression or refresh mostly

For each sampled entry, classify:
- KEEP: already good enough
- PREFIX_ONLY_REWRITE: only the opening phrase needs repair
- FULL_REWRITE_NEEDED: the whole howToFilm is too generic
- CONTENT_RISK: rewriting may change the scene, needs editor attention

Quality standard for repair:
A repaired howToFilm should:
- name the actual object/action in the scene
- tell where to start the shot
- describe the visible movement
- describe the comedic pause/payoff
- stay short, usually 1–2 sentences
- preserve the original whatToShow
- preserve the hook
- preserve the anchor
- preserve safety/privacy
- avoid “phone on tripod” unless it is truly the best instruction

Bad:
“Phone on a tripod, one locked-off shot. Creator scrolls a fake subscription list…”

Better:
“Start close on the fake subscription list. Hold on the tiny charge for one beat, then pull back to your face like the app just became a landlord.”

Do not rewrite yet.
Just audit and propose the repair shape.
