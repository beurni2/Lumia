============================================================
REFERENCE QUALITY BAR
============================================================

Use the post-W2-AUTHOR V2 standard.

`whatToShow` should:
- describe a full visible skit
- include setup, action, turn, payoff
- match the hook
- name the main object/action
- be easy to understand in one read
- feel like a real human moment
- avoid abstract filler
- avoid generic “show yourself reacting” instructions

`howToFilm` should:
- tell the creator how to shoot it
- start with concrete framing or what to keep visible
- describe the key action
- describe the ending/payoff
- be 1–2 sentences
- be shootable in 10–15 minutes
- not repeat `whatToShow` word-for-word
- not introduce a new premise
- not use generic boilerplate

Nigeria-specific quality bar:
- Pidgin/light-pidgin should feel natural, not cartoonish.
- Avoid using “wahala,” “abeg,” “omo,” “jollof,” “NEPA,” “village people,” etc. as lazy shortcuts.
- Cultural details should feel everyday and filmable, not tourist-stereotype.
- The skit should work visually even if someone only lightly understands the slang.
- Humor should come from the human contradiction, not just from Nigerian labels.
- Light pidgin should be light. Do not let it become heavy pidgin unless the cohort asks for pidgin.
