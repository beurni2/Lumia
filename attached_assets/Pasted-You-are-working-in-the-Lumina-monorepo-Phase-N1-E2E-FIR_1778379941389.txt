Root cause:
The full ideator repeats the same top hooks across fresh creators because final selection is greedy and content-only:
- selectWithNovelty calls greedySelect.
- greedySelect picks highest adjustedScore.
- Tie-breaker favors lower pool index.
- hookQualityScore, personalFit, and hookImpact do not use creator id.
- Fresh creators have empty memory, so per-creator novelty penalties do nothing.
- Result: identical-profile fresh creators receive identical sorted pools and same first-card winners.

This also explains Nigerian pack delivery = 0% in E2E:
- Pack gate is true.
- Pack candidates are generated and enter the final pool.
- They lose to the same high-scoring catalog / pattern candidates in greedy selection.
- This is not a probe/source-label bug. It is a real final-selection loss.

Evidence:
- ng_clean: 8 distinct hooks out of 60.
- ng_pidgin: 20 distinct hooks out of 60.
- ng_light_pidgin: 20 distinct hooks out of 60.
- Repeated winners include:
  - the fridge knows i'm lying
  - I am totally fine about the wifi
  - my body quit. my brain kept screaming
  - is it still about the shopping cart
- totally_fine_about_* floods every Nigerian cohort because weak-skeleton quota is currently Western-only.

DESIRED BEHAVIOR:
For first-card selection only:
- Instead of always selecting pool[0], select from a small quality band around the best candidate using stable creator-id hashing.
- Same creator id should always choose the same candidate from the same band.
- Different creator ids may choose different candidates.
- If creator id is missing, fall back to pool[0].
- If band size is 1, fall back to pool[0].
- Only affect the first selected card / first impression slot.
- Do not change selection for subsequent cards unless unavoidable.
- Do not include weak candidates.
