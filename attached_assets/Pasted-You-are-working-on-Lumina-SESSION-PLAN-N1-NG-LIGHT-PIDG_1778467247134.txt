   - The entry sounds closer to strict pidgin than light pidgin.

8. TOO_ENGLISH_FOR_LIGHT_PIDGIN
   - The entry is basically English with one pidgin token pasted in.

9. AIISH_OR_GENERIC
   - The hook feels generic, robotic, fake Nigerian flavor, or low-specificity.

   - The hook/whatToShow is hard to picture or not clearly filmable.
